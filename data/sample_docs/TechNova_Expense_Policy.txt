TechNova Solutions — Travel & Expense Policy

1. Meals
Per diem meal allowance: $60/day for domestic travel, $80/day for international travel. Itemized receipts required above $25.

2. Transport
Economy class for flights under 5 hours; premium economy for flights over 5 hours. Ride-share or public transit preferred over rental cars.

3. Hotels
Standard room at 3-4 star properties. Corporate rate code required. Max nightly rate: $180 domestic, $250 international.

4. Approval
Expenses over $500 require manager pre-approval. All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

5. Software
Subscriptions under $100/month may be expensed directly; above $100/month requires VP approval.
