TechNova Solutions — Employee Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month. Unused annual leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.

3. Public Holidays
TechNova observes 8 paid public holidays per year, published each January by HR.

4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks.

5. Requesting Leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Managers must respond within 2 working days. Emergency leave may be requested by phone to your direct manager.

6. Probation
Employees on probation may take accrued leave only after completing 90 days of service.
