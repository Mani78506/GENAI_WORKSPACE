TechNova Solutions — Information Security Policy

1. Password Complexity Requirements
All passwords must meet the following rules:
- Minimum length: 12 characters
- Must include at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one digit, and one special symbol (!@#$%^&*)
- Must not contain your username, real name, or company name
- Passwords expire every 90 days; the last 5 passwords may not be reused

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all employee accounts. Approved methods: authenticator app (TOTP) or hardware key. SMS codes are not permitted.

3. Workstation Security
Screens must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Devices must run company-managed endpoint protection at all times.

4. Data Handling
Confidential documents may only be shared via approved internal systems. Sending confidential data to personal email accounts is prohibited.

5. Incident Reporting
Suspected security incidents must be reported to security@technova.local within 1 hour of discovery.
