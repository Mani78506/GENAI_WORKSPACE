TechNova Solutions — Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing 6 months of employment, subject to manager approval.

2. Core Hours
All remote employees must be reachable during core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM local time.

3. Equipment
TechNova provides a laptop, monitor, and headset. A $50/month internet stipend is paid to employees working remotely more than 2 days per week.

4. Meetings
Cameras are optional for internal meetings but encouraged for team stand-ups and reviews.

5. Travel
Remote workers within commuting distance must attend the office for quarterly all-hands meetings.
